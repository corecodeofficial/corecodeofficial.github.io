WHY CORECODE

Started on a bench, not in a boardroom.

CoreCode Repairs is a one-person shop built around actually understanding the hardware — tracing a dead power rail instead of swapping the whole board, replacing one burnt component instead of the whole assembly. If it can be fixed at the component level, that's the first option on the table, not the last.

Every job comes with a plain-language explanation of what was wrong and what was done — no jargon, no upsell.
