Full Service List

Four areas. One local shop.

Pricing runs from set rates to labor-based quotes once the fault is diagnosed. Not sure which category fits? Send a description and it'll get sorted out for you.

01

Board-Level & Micro-Soldering Work

  • Port replacements — HDMI, USB-A/USB-C, 3.5mm audio jacks, DC barrel jacks
  • Trace & power rail repair — tracing power issues, fixing torn PCB traces, replacing burnt MOSFETs or micro-capacitors
  • SMD IC component swaps — power management ICs, charging controllers, logic chips
02

Standard Computer & Laptop Hardware

  • Thermal maintenance — full teardown, dust clearing, fresh CPU thermal paste
  • Component upgrades & swaps — installing or replacing any PC component
  • Screen & hinge repair — LCD/OLED panels, digitizers, damaged hinges
  • Custom desktop assembly & modding — full builds, cable management, stress testing
03

Software, Operating System & Data

  • Clean OS installation — Windows, Linux, or ChromeOS Flex, with drivers and updates
  • System cleanup & optimization — malware/virus removal, startup and thermal-throttling fixes
  • Data migration & backup — old or failing drives moved to new NVMe drives
04

Consoles, Peripherals & Custom Hardware

  • Game console repair — HDMI ports, disk drives, overheating, power supplies (PlayStation, Xbox, Switch)
  • Mechanical keyboard modification — switch desoldering/swapping, hot-swap sockets, board repair
  • Custom cable & connector wiring — pinout wiring, USB cable repair, small harnesses
  • Microcontroller & small electronics repair — custom project boards, single-board computers
